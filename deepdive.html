Early career foundations

I started in a fully manual environment with paper job tickets, filing cabinets, low visibility and high error rates. I introduced digital job tracking, indexed document retrieval and structured workflows before this was normal in SME environments.

  • Eliminated lost work and rekeying errors
  • Improved turnaround times and customer experience
  • Created early foundations for scalable operations

What this says about me

Instinct for structureI create control where none exists.
Practical biasI focus on adoptable solutions, not complexity for its own sake.
Flow before toolingControl and process design come before technology embellishment.

Apogee operating backbone

Apogee had fragmented systems and limited control across sales, delivery and service. I designed and built a joined-up operating backbone spanning ERP, CRM, workflow and finance integration.

  • ERP controlled sales through to delivery and sign-off
  • CRM integrated with service, asset and lease data
  • Workflow enforced what good looked like
  • Controls prevented invalid solutions being sold
  • Commercial insight improved visibility of lifecycle, service performance and upgrade opportunity

Tinder from day zero

Before launching Tinder, I built the operating platform first: ERP, CRM, BI, workflows, reporting and dashboards. As the business grew, I evolved it to include mobile workforce integration, compliance gating and Microsoft stack integration.

  • Sustained around 40% year-on-year growth over nearly a decade
  • Real-time visibility across the business
  • High control across a multi-discipline operation

National & FTSE100 accounts

At Apogee I became the go-to technical authority and opinion leader across major national and FTSE100 accounts. I was regularly brought into high-value sales engagements, strategic account reviews and complex solution design sessions.

  • Translated business need into workable solutions
  • Challenged weak assumptions before they became delivery issues
  • Ensured what was sold could actually be delivered at scale

Outcomes

Higher credibilityImproved confidence with major enterprise clients.
Better win qualityImproved solution quality and win rates.
Lower delivery riskReduced the chance of selling something operationally unsound.

Wandsworth Council

A major contract had been sold incorrectly, creating a serious risk of failure and reputational damage. I stepped in, rapidly assessed the constraints, designed a viable solution in real time, delivered within one month and reframed the commercial model so it worked.

First Choice / TUI Group

A large enterprise client was experiencing service and workflow challenges that risked the account. I redesigned the service interaction and support model, introduced better visibility and control, and aligned delivery to operational reality.

  • Stabilised service delivery
  • Improved client confidence
  • Protected a key enterprise account

PE growth and integration

I played a central role in PE funding preparation, board engagement, technical due diligence and day-zero integration. I often stepped into acquired businesses in an MD-style operational leadership role to stabilise operations, protect revenue and embed the Apogee operating model and culture.

  • All acquisitions integrated onto the Apogee platform within 60 days
  • All delivered ROI within 18 months, typically closer to 12
  • 14 acquisitions integrated into a consistent national operating model

What this shows

RepeatabilityI scale through structured methods, not one-off heroics.
Commercial awarenessI combine operational control with ROI discipline.
Hands-on stabilisationI do not just plan integrations, I help land them.

Tinder capability expansion

I led expansion into VoIP and unified comms, managed Wi-Fi, structured cabling, fibre and civils, and passive fire stopping.

  • Rapid learning to working knowledge
  • Partner first, then internalise the capability
  • Build the delivery model before scaling the revenue line

Outcomes

  • Multiple revenue streams created
  • Fast time-to-market, including VoIP live in two months
  • Moved from subcontract to primary delivery in key areas

High-risk civils environment

I operated in live street works and utility-risk environments with frequent audits, potential fines, commercial penalties and HSE exposure. I embedded governance directly into workflows.

  • Compliance gates within ERP workflows
  • Real-time field data capture and validation
  • Audit-ready reporting
  • Alignment to ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and Cyber Essentials expectations

Outcome

  • 92%+ compliance scores
  • Strategic partner status achieved
  • Sole supplier regions awarded
  • Minimal audit failures

Governance model

Across Apogee and Tinder, I introduced structured governance for change, risk, vendor management and operating cadence.

  • Defined change types and approval pathways
  • Active risk registers with ownership and escalation
  • SLA-based supplier governance and review cadence
  • Daily, weekly and monthly operational and leadership rhythms

What this shows

Governance in the flowControl is designed into how work happens.
Cadence mattersGood operating rhythm reduces drift and hidden risk.
Ownership mattersData plus accountability improves response speed and vendor performance.

Walking away from bad revenue

When there was an opportunity to expand Hyperoptic delivery into new regions, I analysed historic and projected data, mapped delivery risk and resource needs, and identified structural unprofitability. I declined the expansion.

  • Avoided overextension
  • Protected core business performance
  • Maintained delivery quality

Strategic exit judgement

When market conditions shifted, consolidation increased and the capital required to compete changed materially, I took a disciplined view. My judgement was that further capital investment would not create an acceptable return or sustainable position.

Commercial realismData over optimism.
Independent judgementWilling to make difficult calls.
Value protectionNot pursuing growth at any cost.
